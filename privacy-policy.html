Privacy Policy

OpportunityTrack privacy policy explaining website logs, contact email, cookies, advertising, Google AdSense disclosures, consent and user choices.

Effective: 30 September 2026•Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how OpportunityTrack may process information when you visit the website, use its search and navigation features, contact the site by email or interact with advertising services that may be enabled. OpportunityTrack is an informational website about scholarships, admissions, internships, fellowships, jobs, education updates and application guidance.

This policy is written to describe the website itself. External universities, scholarship providers, employers, research institutes, government websites and other services linked from OpportunityTrack have their own privacy practices. When you leave OpportunityTrack, review the privacy information provided by the external service if you are going to create an account, submit an application or provide personal information.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you contact OpportunityTrack by email, the information in your message may include your name, email address and anything else you choose to write. The contact form on this website does not upload your message to a custom OpportunityTrack database; it prepares an email for you to send through your own email application. Your email provider and the recipient email service will process the message according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information such as passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, confidential recommendation letters or complete visa files through a general website contact message.

3. Information processed during normal website access

Like most websites, the hosting and security infrastructure used to deliver OpportunityTrack may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, date and time, referring page and security-related request information. This information can be used to deliver pages, maintain reliability, detect abuse and investigate technical problems.

The exact retention and processing performed by the hosting provider depends on the hosting configuration. OpportunityTrack does not use the public pages to ask visitors to create accounts or upload scholarship application documents.

4. Site search and navigation

The built-in OpportunityTrack search feature runs in the visitor’s browser against a small local index of site pages. Search terms typed into that interface are not intentionally sent to a dedicated OpportunityTrack search server by the site JavaScript. Normal hosting logs may still record the pages you open.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

OpportunityTrack itself is designed to work without requiring an account. Some third-party services, particularly advertising services, may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies when they are enabled. These technologies can support functions such as ad delivery, frequency control, security, measurement and user choices.

Your browser allows you to remove or block cookies, although doing so can affect features provided by third parties. Where consent is legally required for advertising or similar storage, the relevant consent mechanism should be presented before the applicable processing takes place.

6. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

OpportunityTrack may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to OpportunityTrack or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Where other third-party advertising vendors or networks are used, their own websites may provide information and choices about personalized advertising.

Advertisements are separate from OpportunityTrack editorial content. The appearance of an advertisement does not mean that OpportunityTrack has evaluated or endorsed the advertiser, service, university, scholarship provider or product shown in the advertisement.

7. Consent for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

When Google advertising is served to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, consent and transparency requirements may apply to the use of cookies or other local storage and to the collection, sharing and use of personal data for advertising. OpportunityTrack may use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform or Google’s Privacy & messaging tools when required for the advertising setup in use.

Consent choices are managed through the consent interface presented to eligible users. Availability and wording can depend on the advertising configuration and applicable region.

8. External links

OpportunityTrack links to third-party websites because applicants need to verify opportunities and complete applications at the official source. Clicking an external link takes you to a service controlled by another organization. OpportunityTrack is not responsible for the content, availability, security or privacy practices of those external sites.

Before entering personal or financial information, confirm that you are on the intended official website and review its privacy notice.

9. Children’s privacy

OpportunityTrack is intended as a general education and career information resource. The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration or application uploads. If a minor is using the site, a parent, guardian or responsible adult should help evaluate external application and privacy requirements where appropriate.

10. Data retention

OpportunityTrack does not maintain a public user-account database through this static website. Email correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to questions, handle corrections, protect the site or maintain records of communications. Technical hosting logs may be retained according to the hosting and security provider’s configuration.

11. Security

Reasonable technical measures can reduce risk but no website or email system can guarantee absolute security. Do not send information that is not necessary for your question. For applications, use the official provider’s secure portal rather than sending sensitive documents to OpportunityTrack.

12. Your choices

You can choose not to contact the site, can manage cookies through your browser and, where relevant, can use the consent controls presented for advertising. Google provides advertising preference controls through Google Ads Settings. If you have a privacy question about information you intentionally sent to OpportunityTrack by email, contact amazaikhan7@gmail.com.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website’s features, advertising setup or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of the page indicates the current version. Material changes should be reflected in the policy before or when the relevant feature is introduced.

14. Contact

Privacy questions about OpportunityTrack can be sent to amazaikhan7@gmail.com. For privacy questions about an external university, scholarship provider, employer, government website or other linked service, contact that organization directly.

OPPORTUNITYTRACK - AUTO-FILL WORKFLOW
====================================

1. Official Sources detects a new item.
2. Open Pending Review.
3. Click "Import & Auto-Fill".
4. OpportunityTrack opens the official page and attempts to fill:
   - Title
   - Category
   - Organization
   - Country / City when clearly available
   - Funding / Type when clearly available
   - Degree / Applicant level
   - Deadline
   - Published date
   - Short description (generated from extracted facts, not copied as an article)
   - Eligibility guidance
   - Full details
   - Application link
   - Official source link
   - Tags
5. The imported item remains PRIVATE as a Draft.
6. Check the information once against the official source.
7. Click "Publish to Site". The system forces status to Published and the fixed public template renders it automatically in the correct category and on its own opportunity URL.

Safety behavior:
- The importer does not invent a deadline, funding amount, country, or other fact if it cannot identify it reliably.
- Missing optional information is allowed; the public template uses clean fallbacks.
- The source URL is always retained so visitors can verify the official provider.

Workflow planning

From discovery to submission: a better application workflow

The final section now uses a card-based workflow instead of one long text block, making the advice easier to scan on both mobile and laptop screens.

01

Build a shortlist

Choose opportunities that match your degree level, field, location flexibility and realistic preparation time.

02

Separate facts from tasks

Keep eligibility, funding and deadlines separate from tasks such as testing, writing and requesting references.

03

Create time buffers

Leave room for translations, referee delays, test scheduling and portal issues so the deadline is not your starting point.

04

Submit honest, specific work

Use credible evidence, avoid copying and explain clearly what you have done, what you want to learn and why the opportunity fits.

Better planning, less confusion

A clearer workflow reduces rushed applications and helps applicants verify the right official route before submitting.
